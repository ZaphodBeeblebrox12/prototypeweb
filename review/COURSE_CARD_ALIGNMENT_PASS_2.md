# Course card and programme positioning — Pass 2

Date: 13 October 2026

## Decisions

- The homepage gives the ₹999 Trading Path the only full, detailed course feature. CONSTRUCT is now a compact next-step teaser; the six-week live classroom stays a brief option row. Visitors can explore the fuller descriptions on the Courses page.
- The homepage's flagship Trading Path card receives a small tilted paper-note label, “a little practice”. The Courses page applies the same paper-note treatment to both detailed course offers, with different wording for CONSTRUCT. The motif is editorial and sparing, not a new badge system.
- CONSTRUCT is described as paper-trading-led, giving participants room to try setups and styles before risking real capital.
- The programme description now makes instructor presence explicit: learners can ask questions in The Wobbling Candle, the instructor will answer them, and live interactions include demonstrations of trade execution and management. No number or calendar cadence for those live lessons is promised in the public copy.
- The live demonstrations are distinguished from participants' own practice: learning execution can happen through live chart walkthroughs while learner experimentation remains paper-trading-led. The programme is still not described as a signal or trade-calls service.
- CONSTRUCT remains ₹17,000, including 12 months of The Wobbling Candle community access with no separate community fee.

## Files updated

- `site/index.html` — only the ₹999 Trading Path retains the full homepage offer card; CONSTRUCT and the live classroom are compact teasers.
- `site/courses.html` — aligned course cards retain detailed comparisons and add the personal note motif; CONSTRUCT copy highlights instructor interactions and paper-trading-led practice.
- `site/construct.html` — updated hero copy, paper-trading/live-execution feature panel, clearer community benefits and FAQ wording.

## Prototype caveat

This is a static website preview. Checkout, enrolment, enquiries, live-session delivery and community access are not connected by this design pass.

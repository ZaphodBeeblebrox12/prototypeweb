# CONSTRUCT integration notes

## Positioning
CONSTRUCT is presented as a guided self-paced 70-day programme, not as a self-study course with no support. Learners study independently during the week, with Monday lesson releases, Sunday assignments, thesis reviews, weekly risk audits, weekly Review Clinics, monthly office-hours AMAs, and a rubric-graded capstone.

## Public pages changed
- `site/courses.html`: now presents the 30-day Trading Path first, CONSTRUCT second, and the existing six-week live classroom as a separate format.
- `site/construct.html`: new programme detail page covering format, 10-week curriculum, Pattern Vault, Ledger, tools, capital note, the separate Income Desk tier, and FAQs.
- `site/index.html`: presents both Trading Path and CONSTRUCT as rich, related course cards; the CONSTRUCT card now communicates the higher-touch support model rather than appearing as a thin promotional strip.
- Public-site pages receive a restrained layered-header treatment using the existing TradeThesis logo and palette. The two core offers share card geometry, spacing and price-panel logic while retaining distinct, offer-specific content and supporting accents.
- `site/00_OPEN_INTERACTIVE_PREVIEW.html`: review router now includes a CONSTRUCT tab.

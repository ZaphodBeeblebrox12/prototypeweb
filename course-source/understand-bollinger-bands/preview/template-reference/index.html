From indicator to judgement

Read what the bands show. Decide what to do next.

Bollinger Bands describe price relative to recent variation. This course teaches the next step: interpreting that evidence without treating every touch, squeeze or close as an automatic signal.

A useful distinction to carry forward

What Are the Bands Showing You?

The bands offer a moving reference for relative price position and recent variation. They do not, on their own, predict the next move.

# Preview production notes

## Purpose and scope

This preview provides a separate place to develop the VPA learner experience while the manuscripts and learning assets continue to be produced in `course-source/volume-price-analysis/`. It is intentionally not part of the public website or course catalogue.

## Learner-facing language

- Use **Lesson rhythm** with the four stages **Notice → Compare → Apply → Review**.
- Replace internal instructional-design terminology such as “Transfer output” with **“What you'll take away.”**
- The default first-module takeaway is **“Evidence observation note.”** Learners should be asked to record visible evidence, a conditional interpretation, a reasonable alternative and what later evidence would strengthen or weaken the reading.
- Explain the purpose in plain language; avoid terms that describe the production process rather than the learner's benefit.

## Duration wording

Display “Estimated lesson time: 12–16 minutes” with the qualifier that this remains a target. The source workflow notes that lesson runtime is not verified until each complete lesson is rehearsed, timed and tested with learners. Do not describe the target as a measured result.

## Verification still required

- Confirm manuscript-to-lesson alignment for all 30 lessons; the preview uses module-level objectives and takeaways to keep the initial scaffold lightweight.
- Ensure each published lesson has its promised worked example, genuine exercise, model reasoning/feedback and knowledge check.
- Rehearse and time complete lessons; conduct beginner readability checks.
- Verify layout, zoom, keyboard/focus use, screen-reader semantics and mobile behavior in an actual browser.
- The existing M01-L01 ChartLab is a pilot asset; its own validation notes identify browser rendering, accessibility and beginner usability checks still to be completed.

## Integration boundary

Do not change the contents of `site/` or the catalogue during this phase. Integrate this course after the manuscript, assets and learner-facing experience have been reviewed and approved. Keep paid-course source files out of public deployment output.

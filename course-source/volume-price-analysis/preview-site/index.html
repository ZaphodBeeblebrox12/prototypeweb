What Candlesticks Don't Tell You: Reading the Volume Behind Price

Separate price outcome from recorded activity. Learn to compare candles with a baseline and state what the data can—and cannot—tell you.

Six modules · 30 lessonsObserve → Compare → Apply → ReviewLight editorial layout
Lesson focus

What you'll learn

Learning objective

Start with what the chart visibly records. Separate that observation from the explanation you might offer, then decide what later evidence could strengthen or weaken the interpretation.

Illustrative price and activitySynthetic teaching example

A teaching illustration, not a trade signal or historical market data. The point of the task is to state visible facts before suggesting an explanation.

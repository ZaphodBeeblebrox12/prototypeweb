# Accessible description — 2.4: Reading Signs of Weakening Pressure

**Teaching focus:** Separate an early warning from an actual structure break and a failed reclaim.

## Panel A — Progress shrinks
Successive pushes gain less distance and close farther from highs. This is a warning, not a confirmed reversal.

## Panel B — Pause then continue
The pullback holds structure and a later push extends the trend. What disproves the early reversal idea?

## Panel C — Break and failed reclaim
Price breaks the swing and cannot reclaim it on the next attempt. Structural evidence is stronger than the first warning.

**Visual key:** Green/red candle bodies indicate close above/below open. Neutral grey columns show recorded activity. The final bar is highlighted; its dashed comparison line is the average of the ten preceding bars. Gold reference line marks the labelled area. Neither colour nor reference guarantees a future direction.

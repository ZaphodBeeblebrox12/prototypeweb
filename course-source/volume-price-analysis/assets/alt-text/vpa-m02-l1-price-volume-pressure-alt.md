# Accessible description — 2.1: Reading Buying and Selling Pressure

**Teaching focus:** Compare price progress with recorded activity. The chart shows behaviour; it does not identify who traded or why.

### Panel A — Progress retained
Higher closes and swing lows show upward progress. Does price keep the progress it makes?

### Panel B — Activity, little progress
Activity increases while closes return to a similar area. What could explain this mismatch?

### Panel C — Downward progress
Lower highs and lower closes show downward progress. What evidence would weaken this reading?

**Visual key:** Green/red candle bodies indicate close above/below open. Neutral grey columns show recorded activity. The highlighted final bar is the case under review; its dashed comparison line represents the average of the ten preceding bars. Gold reference line marks the labelled area. Read the panel's observation and next question independently; the reference is not a guarantee of reversal or continuation.

**Data:** Practice OHLCV series are constructed to produce controlled comparisons for this lesson. The adjacent CSV preserves each open, high, low, close, volume value, ten-bar baseline and relative multiple. No third-party market feed is required to recreate the illustration.

**Readability:** Desktop chart uses a 1600-pixel-wide vector canvas and large labels; mobile chart stacks the cases on a 720-pixel-wide canvas with larger text. SVG text remains vector for zooming.

# Accessible description — 3.3: Read the Response at Support and Resistance

**Teaching focus:** Treat zones as areas to observe, not exact prices that must hold. Distinguish rejection, acceptance and ambiguity.

## Panel A — Rejection develops
Price tests the zone and moves back below it. Look for follow-through away from the zone.

## Panel B — Acceptance beyond
Price holds beyond the zone and makes additional progress. A close outside alone is not the whole sequence.

## Panel C — Ambiguous overlap
Several closes overlap the area without a clean response. Waiting is reasonable until the behaviour clarifies.

**Visual key:** Green/red candle bodies indicate close above/below open. Neutral grey columns show recorded activity. The final bar is highlighted; the dashed comparison line is the average of the ten preceding bars. Gold reference line marks the labelled area. Neither colour nor reference guarantees future direction.

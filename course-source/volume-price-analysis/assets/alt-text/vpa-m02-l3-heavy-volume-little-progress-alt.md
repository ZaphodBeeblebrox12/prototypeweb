# Accessible description — 2.3: Heavy Volume, Little Progress

**Teaching focus:** An activity-versus-result mismatch is a question to investigate, not proof of absorption or hidden intent.

### Panel A — At a prior high
A high-activity candle makes little net progress near resistance. Could this be two-sided trading instead?

### Panel B — After a decline
Activity rises while price stops extending lower for this interval. What would show stabilisation or continued decline?

### Panel C — Event volatility
An activity burst creates a wide range with a mid-range close. Check session, feed and location before inferring cause.

**Visual key:** Green/red candle bodies indicate close above/below open. Neutral grey columns show recorded activity. The highlighted final bar is the case under review; its dashed comparison line represents the average of the ten preceding bars. Gold reference line marks the labelled area. Read the panel's observation and next question independently; the reference is not a guarantee of reversal or continuation.

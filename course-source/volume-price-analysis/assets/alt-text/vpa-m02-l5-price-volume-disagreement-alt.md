# Accessible description — 2.5: When Price and Volume Disagree

**Teaching focus:** Record the mismatch, offer competing explanations, and name the next price behaviour that would distinguish them.

## Panel A — Marginal new high
Price reaches a marginal new high as recorded activity contracts. Check the session baseline and whether structure still holds.

## Panel B — High activity, little result
Activity expands at a boundary, but price does not retain the excursion. What follow-through supports rejection or acceptance?

## Panel C — Quieter decline
Price continues lower while volume bars contract. Low volume does not guarantee that the decline has ended.

**Visual key:** Green/red candle bodies indicate close above/below open. Neutral grey columns show recorded activity. The final bar is highlighted; its dashed comparison line is the average of the ten preceding bars. Gold reference line marks the labelled area. Neither colour nor reference guarantees a future direction.

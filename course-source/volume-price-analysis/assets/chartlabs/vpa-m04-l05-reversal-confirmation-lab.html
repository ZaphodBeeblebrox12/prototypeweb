Reasoning feedback

The first attempt failed to hold, but a broader reversal is not established. Wait for a failed reclaim and meaningful structural change, or a reclaim and hold beyond the boundary.

Remember: a matching outcome does not prove the initial reasoning was sound; evaluate whether the observations and conditions were defensible before the later bars appeared.

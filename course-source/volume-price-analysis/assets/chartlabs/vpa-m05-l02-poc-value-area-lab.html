Finding the Market's Reference Point: Point of Control and Value Area

The Point of Control and Value Area are calculated references in a selected profile—not automatic support, resistance or price targets.

Before you begin: this page shows actual price candles, time-based volume and a horizontal volume-at-price profile. Every example uses synthetic data. A profile summarises the chosen past period; it does not tell you where price must turn.

Level 1 — Notice (easy)

Identify what each display represents. Read the stated profile period, then find the most active price row and the value-area boundaries shown on the chart.

Your task

Locate the POC, VAH and VAL on the chart, and state the period represented by the profile.

The POC is the row with the greatest assigned activity; VAH and VAL bound the platform-defined value area. All three depend on the chosen period and calculation rules.

Path B — return / failure

Holding above VAH and making further progress supports sustained trade beyond prior value. A return inside and failed reclaim weakens that idea. Price being outside does not require a return.

Review evidence available at the time, not simply whether your first guess matched the later move.

Where Has the Market Traded Most? Introducing Volume Profile

A histogram under candles shows activity through time. A Volume Profile groups recorded activity by price over a selected window.

Before you begin: this page shows actual price candles, time-based volume and a horizontal volume-at-price profile. Every example uses synthetic data. A profile summarises the chosen past period; it does not tell you where price must turn.

Level 1 — Notice (easy)

Identify what each display represents. Read the stated profile period, then find the most active price row and the value-area boundaries shown on the chart.

Your task

Identify what the vertical volume bars show and what the horizontal profile shows. Point to one broad price area and one thinner area.

The vertical bars show recorded activity by time interval. The profile distributes activity by price in the selected window. Broad and thin rows describe that dataset; neither forecasts a future reaction.

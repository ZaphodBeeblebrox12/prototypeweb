Build Your Trade Scenario: What Needs to Happen Next?

A scenario is useful when it defines what would support it, what would weaken it and what to do when the evidence does not resolve the question.

How to use this lab: The charts below show price candles and recorded volume. Complete each step before moving on. The response boxes check that you have recorded an answer; they do not automatically judge the quality of your reasoning. These are illustrative teaching cases, not historical market prices.

Reasoning feedback

A strong answer is observable and conditional in both directions. If price keeps overlapping near the boundary, the no-trade outcome is wait; it is not a reason to force a third scenario.

Remember: a matching outcome does not prove the initial reasoning was sound; evaluate whether the observations and conditions were defensible before the later bars appeared.

Show the full model-answer checklist
  • Describe the price result and recorded activity relative to a named comparison.
  • State why the reference zone or location matters.
  • Separate observations from interpretation.
  • Give an alternative explanation.
  • Name future price behaviour that would strengthen or weaken each explanation.
  • Accept “unresolved / wait” when neither outcome has sufficient evidence.

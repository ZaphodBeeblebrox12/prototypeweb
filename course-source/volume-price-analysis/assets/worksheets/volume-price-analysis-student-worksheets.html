1. Context note

Module 6.1 · Start With the Bigger Picture

Show the worked example

State: Price has rotated between roughly 200 and 208 over two sessions.

Location: Near the upper boundary, 208, justified by repeated prior turns.

Event: Price traded above 208 but closed at 207.4 inside the range; recorded activity was elevated against a local baseline.

Scenarios: A close above 208 followed by a successful retest supports continuation. A sustained return inside and failed reclaim supports a range-return reading. Overlap around 207–209 without a trigger means wait.

2. Evidence sheet

Module 6.2 · Read the Evidence

Show the worked example

Price traded above 56.5 but closed at 55.8 inside the prior range; volume was 1.8× the ten-bar average. One reading is a failed attempt to leave the range. An alternative is temporary balance before another attempt. A later hold above the boundary supports continuation; a return inside and failed reclaim weakens it.

4. Pre-trade ticket

Module 6.4 · Plan the Trade Before You Take It

Show the worked calculation (not a recommendation)

Hypothetical entry 54.5, stop 51.5, value 2 currency units per price unit: distance 3.0 × 2 = 6 currency units per unit before costs and slippage. A correct calculation does not approve a trade; the trigger, structural stop, target, instrument rules and personal risk limits still matter.

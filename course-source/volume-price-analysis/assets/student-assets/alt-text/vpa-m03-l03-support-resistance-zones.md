# Accessibility and source notes — M03-L03

**Figure number:** Figure 3.3; interactive panels use Figure 3.3a–Figure 3.3e.
**Lesson:** Trading Near Support and Resistance: What to Look for Before Entry
**Purpose:** Use support/resistance zones as locations to test, not prices that must hold.
**Data:** Synthetic OHLCV created for instruction, not historical prices or a signal.

## Chart description
Price approaches the marked area around 80–82, trades through parts of the zone and then retests it. The example asks the learner to compare rejection, sustained trade beyond the zone and unresolved overlap.

Candlestick price bars appear above the volume bars for the same intervals. Green indicates close above open; red indicates close below open. The dashed line is the recent-bar volume average. The marked zone is Previously tested zone, a context marker, not a guarantee.

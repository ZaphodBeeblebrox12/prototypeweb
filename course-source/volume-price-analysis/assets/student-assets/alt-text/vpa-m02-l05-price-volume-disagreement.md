# Accessibility notes — M02-L05

**Lesson:** When Price and Volume Disagree: Finding Clues Others May Overlook
**Purpose:** Analyse mismatches using alternatives and a future discriminator.
**Source:** Synthetic OHLCV. Not historical data or a trading signal.

**Description:** Price makes a marginal new high while volume falls relative to the prior advance, then prints a high-activity bar with limited progress near the marked high area. The two mismatches raise different questions. The visual places candlestick price bars above matching volume bars. Green bars close above the open; red bars close below. The dashed line is a recent-bar volume average. The marked zone (Prior high area) is context, not a guaranteed reaction.

**Alt text:** When Price and Volume Disagree: Finding Clues Others May Overlook. Synthetic price-and-volume chart. Price makes a marginal new high while volume falls relative to the prior advance, then prints a high-activity bar with limited progress near the marked high area. The two mismatches raise different questions. Compare price progress, activity, location and follow-through.

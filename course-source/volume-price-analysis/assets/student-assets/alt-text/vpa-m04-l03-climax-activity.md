**Lesson:** When Volume Explodes at Market Extremes: Understanding Climax Activity
**Purpose:** Interpret an exceptional volume bar using price result, location and later behaviour.
**Data:** Synthetic OHLCV, not historical prices or a trading signal.

## Chart description
An extended upward move reaches the prior high area. Activity spikes sharply around the focus event, but the close retains little additional progress. The outcome is deliberately not predetermined by the volume spike.

Candlestick bodies and wicks show price; aligned volume bars show recorded activity. The dashed line compares volume with recent bars. The highlighted/shaded zone is Prior high area. Read the event in the context of the boundary and following bars.

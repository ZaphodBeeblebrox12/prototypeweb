## Primary chart: When Price Moves on Low Volume: Opportunity or Warning Sign?
Lower recorded activity during a pullback can be a clue, but it does not prove the pullback has finished.

The chart has a labelled price pane above a volume pane. Each candle shows an open/close body and high/low wick; green candles close above their open and red candles close below their open. Volume bars align with the corresponding price bar. A dashed horizontal line shows an average of recent bars; the shaded dashed range identifies Prior breakout zone. Exact values are available in the matching CSV.

## Chart reading notes
An upward impulse is followed by a pullback toward the dashed reference area. Volume decreases during the retracement, then price begins to stabilise. The visual supports a question about the pullback's result; it does not guarantee trend continuation.

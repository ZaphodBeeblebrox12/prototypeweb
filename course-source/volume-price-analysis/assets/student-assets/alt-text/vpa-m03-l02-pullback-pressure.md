**Lesson:** Is the Pullback Running Out of Steam? Reading Moves Against the Trend
**Purpose:** Compare pullback depth, activity, location and structural result against the prior impulse.
**Data:** Synthetic OHLCV for teaching, not historical prices or a trading signal.

## Chart description
An upward impulse from 100 to 116 is followed by a pullback toward the former breakout zone around 112–114. Activity declines during the retreat, but price has not yet produced an independent upward trigger.

Candlestick price bars appear above matching volume bars. Green candles close above open, red close below open. Dashed lines show recent volume average and the marked area is Prior breakout zone. The zone frames a question; it is not a guaranteed support/resistance level. The corresponding CSV contains all staged cases.

**Lesson:** Before You Enter: Separating Promising Setups From Weak Ones
**Purpose:** Assess context, location, evidence, trigger, invalidation and practical risk before considering a trade.
**Data:** Synthetic OHLCV for learning, not historical prices or a signal.

## Chart description
Price advances, pulls back into the possible setup area and then moves higher. The chart is useful only if the area has a reason, the later trigger is observable and the invalidation/risk can be stated.

The chart shows candle wicks/bodies and matching volume bars. Green indicates a close above open; red indicates a close below open. Dashed horizontal lines mark a recent-bar volume average and the zone Possible setup area. The zone marks a question, not a guaranteed reaction.

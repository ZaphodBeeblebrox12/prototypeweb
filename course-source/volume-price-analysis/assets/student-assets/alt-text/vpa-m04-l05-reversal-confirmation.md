# Accessibility and source notes — M04-L05

**Lesson:** Before Calling a Reversal: What to Look for After the Breakout
**Purpose:** Separate an initial failed breakout from stronger evidence of a reversal in price structure.
**Data:** Synthetic OHLCV; not historical market data or a trading recommendation.

## Chart description
Price advances toward and above a reference boundary, then returns inside. The next bars fail to reclaim the area and break nearby structure, building a stronger downside scenario than the original excursion alone.

The chart shows price candles with wicks and close bodies, with matching recorded-volume bars beneath. The dashed price zone is Range boundary; it marks prior context rather than a guaranteed reversal. The learner should compare the first excursion with the later reclaim and swing-structure evidence.

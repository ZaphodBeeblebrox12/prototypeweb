## Chart description
Price rotates near the 206–208 range high. It briefly travels above the boundary but returns inside on the focus event. Continuation and range-return scenarios are both plausible until later evidence separates them.

The chart includes candlesticks and aligned volume bars. Dashed labelled price references mark Range high. The levels are a teaching illustration only. Stop execution, costs, slippage and instrument specifications can change actual risk.

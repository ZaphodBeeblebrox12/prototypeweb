# Accessibility and source notes — M02-L02

**Figure number:** Figure 2.2; interactive panels use Figure 2.2a–Figure 2.2e.
**Linked lesson:** When Volume Supports a Move: Recognising Evidence of Strength
**Purpose:** Combine breakout close, relative activity and follow-through without treating volume as confirmation alone.
**Data:** Synthetic OHLCV created for instruction, not historical market data, a forecast or trading signal.

## What the chart shows
Price advances towards and beyond the marked range-high area. The late bars have elevated activity and retain progress above the zone; the contrast case will show a similarly active attempt returning inside.

The visual has a price pane with candle bodies and high/low wicks above volume bars for the same intervals. Green means the candle closed above its open; red means it closed below its open. A dashed line shows the average volume of recent bars. The shaded/dashed price zone is **Prior range high**, a context marker rather than a guaranteed reaction point. Values for all stages are in the matching CSV.

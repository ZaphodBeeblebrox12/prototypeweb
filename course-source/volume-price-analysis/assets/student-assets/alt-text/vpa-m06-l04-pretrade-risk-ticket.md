# Accessibility and source notes — M06-L04

**Lesson:** Plan the Trade Before You Take It
**Purpose:** Separate thesis and trigger from risk control, position size, objective and management rules.
**Data:** Synthetic OHLCV, not historical prices, a forecast or trade recommendation.

## Chart description
The chart shows a possible setup area, a trigger line above it, an invalidation/stop reference below the recent structure and an illustrative objective above current price. These lines demonstrate planning geometry, not a recommended trade.

The chart includes candlesticks and aligned volume bars. Dashed labelled price references mark Setup area; Trigger, Invalidation/stop and Illustrative objective lines are explicitly labelled. The levels are a teaching illustration only. Stop execution, costs, slippage and instrument specifications can change actual risk.

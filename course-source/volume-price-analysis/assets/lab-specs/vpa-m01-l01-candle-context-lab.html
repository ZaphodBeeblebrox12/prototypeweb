Notice. Compare. Apply.

You'll start with a simple observation, compare two similar candles, then work through a new breakout case before seeing what happened next. Each stage asks a little more of you. This is practice in reading evidence—not a trading signal.

LEVEL 3 OF 3 · INDEPENDENT APPLICATION

Make a reading before the outcome is known

This is a new case. Price has moved above a prior range high on elevated volume. Before seeing later bars, record the visible evidence, two possible explanations and what would help you tell them apart.

Compare the two outcomes

The first breakout candle was identical in both cases. The difference is the price behaviour that followed—not a change in what the first candle showed.

Alternative ending A

Price holds outside the range and makes further upward progress.

Alternative ending B

Price returns below the range high and makes further downward progress.

Compare your notes with a model reading

What was visible before the reveal: price closed above the prior range high. Reported volume was 410 compared with a five-bar baseline of 184, about 2.23 times that baseline.

Continuation is one plausible explanation: price may hold above the former range high and continue making upward progress.

Failure is another plausible explanation: price may return inside the range, fail to regain the boundary and then make downward progress.

The key distinction: high activity makes the event worth examining; it does not decide between the two explanations. The later price response supplies the discriminating evidence. A strong reading describes the facts, gives both scenarios and names what would support or weaken each—without claiming to know the participants' motives.

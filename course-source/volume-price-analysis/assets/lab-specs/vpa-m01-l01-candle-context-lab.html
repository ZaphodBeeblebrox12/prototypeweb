Read Volume. Find Trade Setups. · Module 1 · Lesson 1.1

The same candle. Different context.

This practice is about reading what is visible—not predicting the next candle. The chart below is synthetic and is not historical market data.

Your task

Study both panels. Before you decide what they may mean, describe what price was doing before each candidate candle, where the candle appears, and how the last volume bar compares with the dashed baseline (the average of the five bars before it).

Remember: a candle is an observation, not a complete explanation. Give yourself time to record the evidence before reading the model answer.

Separate what price did from what you think it may mean.

Notice both location and activity, not candle colour alone.

Self-check

Before opening the model answer, check each point that your written response covers.

Reveal model reasoning

Panel A

Price advanced, pulled back from a prior swing high and then printed a green candle during a recovery. The latest volume is only modestly above the average of the previous five bars. One possible reading is that the pullback may be pausing; an alternative is that the recovery may fail. Watch whether price regains a nearby short-term high, holds the pullback area or instead breaks lower.

Panel B

Price advanced for a longer sequence and is testing an area near prior highs. The candle is similarly proportioned, but activity is clearly above the five-bar baseline. This deserves attention, but does not prove a top or identify who is trading. Further progress and holding beyond the area would support one scenario; a stall, return below the area and failed reclaim would support another.

What matters

The candle shapes are similar, but location and relative activity differ. The next bars help distinguish the possible readings. A strong answer explains the evidence, offers an alternative and states what would change its mind.

# Day 30 — Final Quiz & Graduation 🎓

**🎬 Thirty days ago, you didn't know what a bid was**

Think back to Day 1. The Flash Crash — Accenture at one cent, PG&E at $39,999 — was
probably your first time hearing that a price is just the last agreement between two
people. You didn't know a limit order from a stop. You'd never heard of Nick Leeson,
LTCM, the Turtles, or the day oil went below zero. The March 2020 hammer, Bitcoin's
$10k wall, Meta's $230 billion single day, Kerviel's €4.9 billion spiral — all of it
was someone else's story.

Now you carry thirty of these stories — not as trivia, but as installed instincts. When
you see a level rejected a fourth time, you'll think of $10,000 Bitcoin. When you feel
the urge to widen a stop, you'll think of a 233-year-old bank. When you're tempted to
size up after three wins, you'll think of a quiet man in Paris who couldn't take ten
minutes. That's what 30 days of deliberate practice does: it converts history into
reflexes.

One last test remains. Fifteen questions across everything. Score 12+ to graduate and
unlock your Strategy Playbook PDF and certificate.

💬 **One last question before everything ends:** thirty days of stories — from the Flash Crash to the dabbawalas. Which single story do you think you'll actually remember during your first live losing week? Note it. Whatever you picked — that's your anchor. Now go prove the lessons stuck. 🎓

## FINAL QUIZ (15 questions)

**1.** Price moves because…
a) news is released  b) aggressive buyers outnumber sellers (or vice versa)  c) indicators cross

a) ₹4,500  b) ₹6,000  c) ₹450

**3.** A level touched and held 5 times over 3 months is…
a) strong  b) increasingly likely to break  c) irrelevant

**4.** The best first target for a long out of a base is…
a) +10R always  b) the next structural obstacle  c) wherever it feels high

**5.** In an uptrend, RSI dipping to 45 and turning up is…
a) a sell signal  b) a pullback buy zone  c) noise

**6.** Account ₹4,00,000. Risk 1.5%. Max rupee risk per trade?
a) ₹4,000  b) ₹6,000  c) ₹60,000

**7.** Wins 35%, avg win +3R; losses 65%, −1R. Expectancy?
a) +0.4R  b) +0.05R  c) −0.6R

**8.** Your 5-minute chart shows a perfect short; the daily is a raging uptrend. You…
a) short small  b) ignore the 5m — trade with the daily or wait  c) flip a coin

**9.** A breakout candle closes beyond the range high on 3× volume. This is…
a) confirmed  b) a fakeout  c) irrelevant without the close

**10.** The four stop sins include all EXCEPT…

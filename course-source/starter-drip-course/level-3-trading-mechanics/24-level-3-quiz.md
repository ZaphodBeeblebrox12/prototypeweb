# Day 24 — Level 3 Quiz 🎓

Before the questions, one last story — because it ties this whole level together.

In 2012, a team of researchers studied every day trader on the Taiwan Stock Exchange
over 15 years — millions of accounts. The finding should be tattooed on every trading
terminal: **fewer than 1% of day traders were predictably profitable after costs.** Not
1% great, not 1% lucky — 1% *predictably* profitable. The other 99% were the casino
patrons from Day 21, feeding the house with frequency.

Now notice what this level just installed in you: defined entries and exits (Day 17),
structural stops (18), the 1–2% rule (19), formula sizing (20), expectancy math (21),
one entry family mastered (22), and a written checklist (23). Every single one of those
is a filter that removes you from the 99% and moves you toward the 1%. The quiz below
proves the filters are loaded. 🎓

💬 **Before the questions:** on a scale of 1–10, how ready do you *feel*? Note the number. After the quiz, compare it to your actual score. That gap — between felt-readiness and real-readiness — is one of the most expensive gaps in trading, and today you get to measure yours for free.

## 🇮🇳 The Indian story: the number SEBI published

Before the quiz, pin this to your wall: SEBI studied every individual F&O trader in
India across FY22–FY24 and found **93% lost money**, the average loser gave up about
**₹2 lakh**, and the system's total retail loss approached **₹1.8 lakh crore** — with
brokers and government collecting tens of thousands of crores in charges along the way.
Taiwan's 1% and India's 93% are the same statistic measured from opposite ends: almost
nobody survives active trading without process. The twelve questions below check whether
your process is installed. Taiwan counted its day traders; SEBI counted yours. Pass this
quiz, and you become the exception both regulators measured.

# 📊 Chart Patterns — trigger, target, volume check

| Pattern | Type | Trigger (trade ONLY this) | Measured target | Volume requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Double top (M)** | Reversal | Neckline break (below middle low) | Neckline − pattern height | Break on expansion; 2nd top high volume + stall = extra bearish |
| **Double bottom (W)** | Reversal | Neckline break (above middle high) | Neckline + pattern height | Break on expansion |
| **Head & shoulders** | Reversal | Neckline break | Neckline ± head-to-neckline height | Right shoulder on declining volume |
| **Bull flag / pennant** | Continuation | Break of flag's upper edge | Break + pole height | Flag drift on LOW volume |
| **Symmetrical triangle** | Either | Close beyond either edge | Break + triangle height at base | Expansion on break — half size if absent |
| **Ascending/descending triangle** | Continuation (usually) | Break of the FLAT side (3+ holds) | Break + triangle height | Flat-side defended on declining volume |

**Universal rules**
1. Never anticipate — the neckline/boundary break IS the trade (or its retest).
2. Weak-volume breakout → likely fakeout → demand the close + volume, or trade the
   FAILURE back inside (often the best entry of all).
3. Always check one timeframe up — patterns against the HTF trend fail constantly.

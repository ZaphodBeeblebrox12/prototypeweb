# 📖 Trading Glossary — plain English, Indian market context

**Ask** — lowest price a seller currently accepts. You buy AT the ask.
**Bid** — highest price a buyer currently offers. You sell AT the bid.
**Spread** — bid↔ask gap; a toll paid on every market-order trade.
**Slippage** — difference between expected and actual fill price; worst in fast markets.
**Liquidity** — how much trades without moving price; high = tight spreads, clean exits.
**Lot size** — minimum tradable quantity per futures/options contract (e.g., Nifty 75).
**Margin** — money your broker blocks to open a leveraged position.
**Leverage** — controlling a large position with small capital; amplifies BOTH directions.
**Mark-to-market** — daily cash settlement of futures P&L; losses debited same day.
**MTF / Multiple Timeframe Analysis** — Bias (daily) → Setup (1H) → Trigger (15m).
**R-multiple (R)** — profit/loss measured in units of initial risk. +2R = won 2× risked.
**Expectancy** — average R per trade; positive after costs = you have an edge.
**Drawdown** — peak-to-trough account decline; the survival metric.
**Circuit breaker (trading)** — your personal daily stop: 3 losses or −3% → done.
**Stop hunt** — quick poke beyond a level to trigger stops before reversing; why zones
have width and stops sit beyond them.
**Fakeout / false breakout** — breakout that instantly reverses back inside the range.
**Confluence** — 2+ independent tools pointing at the same price = higher-probability zone.
**Pullback** — temporary counter-move within a trend; the discount entry.
**Breakout** — price closing beyond a defined boundary (range, pattern neckline).
**Neckline** — the boundary whose break confirms a double top/bottom or H&S.
**Measured move** — pattern target = breakout point ± pattern height.
**Disposition effect** — cutting winners early + holding losers; loss aversion in action.
**Revenge trading** — immediate, oversized re-entry after a loss. Account-killer #1.
**Averaging down** — adding to a losing position. Forbidden.
**Pyramiding** — adding only to winning positions, per pre-written plan. Allowed.
**Forward test** — trading a validated system at minimum size live before scaling.
**Backtest** — verifying rules against historical data before risking money.
**ATM / ITM / OTM** — option strike position vs current price (At/In/Out of the money).
**Premium** — the price you pay to buy an option; can go to zero.
**Theta decay** — options lose value as expiry approaches; the buyer's silent tax.
**FII / DII** — Foreign / Domestic Institutional Investors; the big money.
**Open Interest (OI)** — outstanding contracts; rising OI with rising price = fresh longs.
**PCR (Put-Call Ratio)** — sentiment gauge; extremes flag crowded positioning.

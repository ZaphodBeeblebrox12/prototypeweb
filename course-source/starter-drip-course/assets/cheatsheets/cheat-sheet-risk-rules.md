# 🛡️ The Risk Card — the 5 rules that keep you alive

```
1. RISK 1–2% per trade (1.5% default). Sized by formula, never by feeling.
2. CORRELATED = ONE TRADE. Nifty + BankNifty long = one directional bet.
3. FREQUENCY CAP. Max 3 trades/day (intraday) · 5–8 open positions (swing).
4. CIRCUIT BREAKER. 3 straight losses OR −3% on the day → terminal CLOSED.
5. REVERSE CHECK every order: size × stop distance × value/point ≤ risk budget.
```

**Tilt protocol:** after ANY loss → 10 minutes away from screen, mandatory.
After 3 wins in a row → halve size until the checklist says normal. Wins are the
danger zone.

**Recovery math reminder:** −50% needs +100%. Survival first, profits second.

# 📈 Indicator Quick-Rules — 20/50/200 · RSI · MACD · Fibonacci

## Moving Averages
- **Above rising 200** → longs only. **Below falling 200** → shorts only. This filter
  alone deletes most beginner losses.
- Strong uptrend pullbacks bounce off **20 EMA** (sometimes 50). Failed bounce = warning.
- **Golden/death cross = regime info, NOT entry.** Months late.
- Max 3 MAs on a chart. More lines = more excuses.

## RSI (14)
- **Uptrend regime:** dips to **40–50** and turn up = buy zone. RSI 70+ is normal in trends.
- **Downtrend regime:** rallies to **50–60** and roll over = sell zone.
- ❌ Never short "overbought" RSI in a strong trend. Ever.
- **Divergence:** price new high + RSI lower high → tighten stops, wait for structure
  break. Warn first, trade the confirmation.

## MACD
- **Zero line** = momentum regime (above = bullish vote).
- **Histogram shrinking** while price rises = momentum leaking → protect profits.
- Crossovers: trust ONLY at the zero line, with trend. In ranges = spam.

## Fibonacci
- Draw from **true swing extremes** (wicks), in trend direction: low→high for uptrend
  retracements.
- **Golden zone = 50–61.8%** — high-quality pullback termination band.
- ❌ Never trade Fib alone: grade by confluence — Fib + MA/zone/trendline = A-grade;
  Fib floating alone = no trade.
- Targets: 127.2% / 161.8% extensions, confirmed by measured moves (Day 12).

## The confluence grade (works for everything)
2+ independent tools at one price = A-grade zone · 1 tool = B-grade, half size ·
0 tools = no trade.

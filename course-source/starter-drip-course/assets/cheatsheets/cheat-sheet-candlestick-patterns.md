# 🕯️ Candlestick Patterns — the only 5 worth knowing

**Golden rule: pattern + level + trend = signal. Pattern alone = noise.**

| Pattern | Looks like | Says | Entry | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pin bar** | Long wick (2–3× body), tiny body at one end | Pushed one way, violently rejected | Break of pin's extreme (direction of wick) | Beyond the other end |
| **Engulfing** | Body swallows previous body, opposite color | Full control seized | Break of engulfing extreme | Beyond engulfing's far end |
| **Inside bar** | Entire candle inside previous range | Volatility contraction | Break of MOTHER candle's high/low (with trend) | Beyond mother candle's other side |
| **Doji** | Open ≈ close, cross-like | Indecision — ONLY valid at extremes after extended moves | Break of doji's high/low | Beyond doji's other end |
| **Morning/Evening star** | Strong → tiny stall → strong opposite | Momentum handover | Break of 3rd candle's extreme | Beyond the pattern extreme |

**Quick grading:** at a level + with trend = A-grade · at a level against trend = half
size · mid-range anywhere = no trade.

**Rejection reading:** long upper wick = sellers won the fight · long lower wick =
buyers won · body at one end + wick at the other = the wick's side lost.

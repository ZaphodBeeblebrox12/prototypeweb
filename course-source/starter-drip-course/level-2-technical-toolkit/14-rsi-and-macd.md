# Day 14 — RSI & MACD: The Two Workhorses (and Their Traps)

**🎬 The divergence that called the top of the crypto mania**

Bitcoin made its first major top on **April 14, 2021, near $64,800** — the Coinbase
listing euphoria. It crashed 53% by May, recovered over the summer, and then in November
made a *new all-time high*: about **$69,000** on November 10, 2021. The headlines were
ecstatic — new high! The bulls were right all along!

But look under the hood at the RSI. In April, momentum peaked with price. In November,
price made a higher high — and RSI made a clearly **lower high**. The market needed more
and more buying effort to produce less and less upward progress. Under the surface,
momentum was dying. That single divergence was the last warning before a bear market
that took Bitcoin to roughly **$15,500** — a **−77%** decline.

Meanwhile, remember May 19, 2021? RSI had screamed "oversold" days *before* the crash
bottomed — and kept screaming while price fell another 30%. Traders who treated
"oversold" as a buy signal got run over by a train going downhill.

Both stories in one asset, within six months. That is today's lesson: indicators don't
lie — beginners just ask them the wrong questions.

> 💬 **The trap test:** Bitcoin made a brand-new all-time high in November 2021 — higher than ever! If you'd seen only the price chart, what would you have thought? "To the moon" or "something's off"? Note your gut call — then learn what the buyers at the top never saw.

## What you'll learn
- What RSI really measures (it is not "overbought/oversold")
- MACD as a momentum regime meter
- Divergences: the single best use of both

## RSI — the momentum speedometer

RSI (14-period) measures the *speed and size of recent gains vs recent losses*, 0–100.

### The trap: "RSI 70 = sell"

In a strong uptrend, RSI lives at 60–80 for weeks. In 2020–21, Bitcoin's uptrend
spent months with RSI pinned "overbought" while price tripled. **Overbought is not a
sell signal in a trend — it's a feature of one.** The May 2021 "oversold" buyers
learned the mirror-image lesson.

### The three correct uses

**1. Regime reading (the best one):**
- Uptrend: RSI dips to **40–50** and turns up = trend healthy = the pullback buying zone.
- Downtrend: rallies to **50–60** and roll over = downside healthy.
- **In uptrends buy the 40–50 dip, don't sell the 70 reading.**

**2. Divergence (the November 2021 lesson):**
Price makes a new high, RSI makes a *lower* high → momentum fading. It doesn't time the
top (nothing does) — it ends your aggression and tightens stops. Then the structure
break does the timing.

**3. Failure swings** (advanced) — note them, master the first two uses first.

## MACD — momentum's center of gravity

MACD = 12-EMA minus 26-EMA, with a 9-EMA signal; the **histogram** shows acceleration.

1. **Zero line = regime.** Above = bullish momentum vote — a second filter beside the
   200 MA. Two agreeing filters beat one.
2. **Histogram turns = momentum shifts.** Histogram shrinking for days while price
   still rises = momentum leaking out → protect profits (exactly what BTC's histogram
   did into November 2021).
3. **Crossovers = the noisy layer**, built from averages of averages — lagging. Trust
   them only **at the zero line**, with trend.

## The divergence playbook (your #1 takeaway from today)

1. Price makes new high after a long run → check RSI.
2. RSI makes a *lower* high → note it, tighten trailing stop. **Do not short yet.**
3. Price breaks its last swing low while RSI stays elevated → confirmation; now the
   pullback trade is on.

> Divergences warn (November warned). Structure breaks confirm. You trade the
> confirmation, protected by the warning.

> ⚠️ **Common beginner mistakes**
> 1. Shorting overbought RSI in a strong uptrend (the classic account-killer).
> 2. Trading raw MACD crossovers in ranges — the histogram whipsaws you to death.
> 3. Hunting divergences on the 5-minute chart — divergences are a higher-timeframe
>    tool (4H/daily minimum). The November 2021 signal was on the *daily*.

## 🇮🇳 The Indian story: the October 2021 divergence that cost Nifty 17%

India got its own version of the November 2021 crypto divergence — six months earlier.
Nifty made its then-all-time high of **18,604 in October 2021**, with RSI printing a
lower high versus the September peak — momentum fading under new price highs. Then, on
the daily, Nifty started making lower highs through January 2022 while breadth
deteriorated — the structure break confirming the warning. Over the next eight months,
Nifty fell to roughly **15,200 by June 2022, about −17%**, in a year when most global
markets fell in tandem. The divergence didn't predict the Ukraine war or the Fed hikes —
it simply told attentive traders the rally's fuel was gone. Warned in October,
confirmed in January, paid by June. Same playbook as Bitcoin, same year, your market.

## Key takeaways
- RSI is a momentum speedometer, not an overbought alarm. In trends, 40–50 dips are buy
  zones; 70+ is normal.
- MACD zero line + histogram = regime and warning layer; crossovers rank last.
- Divergence (price vs RSI/MACD) is your best early-warning system — it called the
  November 2021 crypto top months before the crowd believed it.

# ❓ Course FAQ (use on your sales page)

**How is the course delivered?**
Instantly after payment you get the complete course pack: a **PDF ebook** and a
**mobile-friendly HTML edition** you can read on any phone, tablet, or laptop. On top of
that, lessons are **dripped to your email — one per day for 30 days** — so you actually
finish instead of binge-reading Day 1 and forgetting the rest.

**How much time do I need daily?**
15–20 minutes for the lesson + 10 minutes for the action step. The daily pace is designed
for working professionals — read it with your morning chai, before the market opens, or
after it closes.

**Can I do this on a phone?**
Yes. The HTML edition reads beautifully on any phone. For chart practice, the free
TradingView mobile app is enough until Level 3.

**Which market does this cover?**
The principles are market-agnostic and every example uses Indian instruments (Nifty,
Bank Nifty, NSE stocks, futures) alongside global stories (COVID crash, Bitcoin, oil,
Archegos) so you learn both lenses. All risk math is in rupees.

**Is this a calls/signals service?**
No — and that's the point. You'll learn to find, enter, manage, and review your own
trades. Signals make you dependent; this makes you self-sufficient. (Graduates often join
the main channel to watch these exact methods applied live.)

**Do I need a demat account to start?**
Not until Level 3's action steps. Levels 1–2 are fully learnable with a free charting app.

**How long do I have access?**
Lifetime. The 30-day drip is the pace, not a deadline — the full pack is yours from Day 1,
and the emails just keep you honest.

**Are there live classes?**
No — by design. Live classes don't scale, and recordings age. The lessons, quizzes,
cheat sheets, and action steps are the whole course. Questions? Just reply to any drip
email — a human reads them.

**What if I'm a complete beginner?**
Perfect. Level 1 assumes zero knowledge — Day 1 starts from "what is a price."

**I've traded before and lost money. Is this too basic?**
Traders who lose consistently almost always have gaps in Level 3 (mechanics) and Level 4
(psychology) — not in indicators. The 50-question practice quiz bank lets you test
yourself before deciding.

50-question quiz bank, First-30-Days-Live plan, 10 trade review cards, playbook template,
and completion certificate.

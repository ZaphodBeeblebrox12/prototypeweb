# 🎁 BONUS 5 — 10 Annotated Trade Review Cards

Ten classic setups as "cards": context → trigger → management → lesson. Five cards map to
charts you already export for the lessons (marked REUSE) — no extra TradingView work.
Cards 6–10 are text-only walkthroughs you can build charts for later (optional).

## Card 1 — The Trendline Pullback (REUSE: L2-10 chart)
- **Context:** Daily uptrend (above 200 MA), 1H trendline with 3 touches.
- **Trigger:** Price returns to the line; 1H bullish engulfing forms ON the line.
- **Management:** Stop below the pullback low. Target: prior high (2.3R). Book half at +2R,
  trail rest under 1H swing lows.
- **Lesson:** The third touch of a confirmed trendline + a Level-2 pattern = the
  highest-probability entry in the entire course.

## Card 2 — The Measured Move (REUSE: L2-12 chart)
- **Context:** Nifty daily double bottom, neckline at 24,000, height 150 points.
- **Trigger:** Neckline break candle on 2× volume.
- **Management:** Entry 24,010, stop below breakout candle low (risk 70), target 24,150
  (≈2R). Exit fully at measured move — ranges pay what they promise, not more.
- **Lesson:** Pattern + trigger + measured target + volume = a complete plan in one candle.

## Card 3 — The Divergence Warning (REUSE: L2-14 chart)
- **Context:** Extended daily rally; price makes a marginal new high.
- **Signal:** RSI makes a clear lower high (divergence).
- **Management:** No short yet. Existing longs: tighten trail. New entries: suspended.
  When the last 1H swing low breaks → pullback trade shorts become legal.
- **Lesson:** Divergences don't time tops; they end your aggression. The structure break
  does the timing.

## Card 4 — The Golden Zone Pullback (REUSE: L2-15 chart)
- **Context:** Strong daily impulse; pullback enters 50–61.8% Fib band AT the 50 EMA.
- **Trigger:** 15m pin bar rejecting the band's lower edge.
- **Management:** Stop below the 78.6% (beyond the golden zone = idea dead). Targets:
  prior high, then 127.2% extension.
- **Lesson:** Three independent tools (Fib + MA + zone) agreeing at one price is what
  "confluence" looks like in the wild.

## Card 5 — Breakout vs Pullback, Same Move (REUSE: L2-22 chart)
- **Context:** Range compression after a trend day; both entries available.
- **Version A (breakout):** close beyond range high, stop inside range. Result: +1.4R
  (paid worst price, survived a fakeout poke).
- **Version B (pullback):** waited for retest of broken range high as support, engulfing
  trigger. Result: +2.6R (paid best price, needed patience).
- **Lesson:** Same move, two R values. The market pays patience at different rates.

## Card 6 — The Fakeout That Was the Trade
- **Context:** Triangle break above on modest volume; two candles later, a violent
  bearish engulfing closes back INSIDE the triangle.
- **Play:** The failure itself is the signal — short the engulfing, stop above the fake
  breakout high. Every trapped breakout buyer becomes fuel.
- **Lesson:** Failed setups are information. Sometimes they're the best setup on the board.

## Card 7 — The News Blackout Save
- **Context:** RBI policy day, 9:55 AM. Your setup fires at a level — 10 minutes before
  the announcement.
- **Play:** Skip. Rule 26 exists because slippage makes stops fiction during news.
  The stock gapped 4% through where your stop "would have" been.
- **Lesson:** The trade you skip is the cheapest trade you'll ever take.

## Card 8 — The Circuit Breaker Day
- **Context:** Three stopped trades by 11:30 AM. Frustration high; a "perfect" setup
  appears at 12:15.
- **Play:** Terminal closed. The 12:15 setup faked out and fell 2% — it was a trap day.
- **Lesson:** Your circuit breaker isn't a cost; it's a filter for days your edge is absent.

## Card 9 — The Overconfidence Trade
- **Context:** Five straight wins, journal says "feeling sharp." Position sized 2× formula
  "just this once."
- **Result:** One loss = two weeks of profits gone. Checklist box #6 skipped ("I sized
  it in my head").
- **Lesson:** Wins are when process matters MOST. The journal field "checklist followed?"
  exists precisely for this trade.

## Card 10 — The Journal Payoff
- **Context:** Monthly review, 42 trades logged. Sort by setup: "Pullback-20EMA"
  expectancy +0.31R over 18 trades; "random momentum" trades −0.4R over 9.
- **Play:** Next month: pullback system only. Results improve within two weeks — same
  skill, better filtering.
- **Lesson:** Your journal's setup column IS your edge-discovery machine. Log honestly,
  sort monthly, delete the negative rows from your behavior.

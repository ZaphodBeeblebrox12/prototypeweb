# 🎁 BONUS 3 — 15 Trading Myths That Drain Indian Trading Accounts

| # | Myth | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You need big capital to start." | You need survival rules. ₹1 lakh with 1% risk beats ₹10 lakh with 10% risk — every time, mathematically. |
| 2 | "High win rate = profits." | Expectancy decides. A 40% win rate at 1:3 out-earns a 70% win rate at 1:0.7. |
| 3 | "RSI above 70 means sell." | In trends RSI lives at 60–80 for weeks. 70 is a feature of strength, not a sell signal. |
| 4 | "Stop losses make me lose money." | No stop is how accounts die in one day. Stops make losses small, survivable, and forgettable. |
| 5 | "More indicators = better analysis." | More indicators = more contradictions. 2–3 tools with confluence beat 12 fighting each other. |
| 6 | "I must trade every day." | Overtrading pays the spread and charges 300× a quarter. Three A-grade trades a week is a career. |
| 7 | "Option buying is safe — risk is limited to premium." | Per-unit risk is capped at 100% — which is exactly what premium decay delivers most weeks. |
| 8 | "Intraday is the fast way to grow a small account." | Costs + slippage + noise tax frequency. Swing trading scales better for busy people — and most pros. |
| 9 | "Averaging down is an investing strategy." | In leveraged trading it's doubling down on being wrong, with margin interest. Forbidden. |
| 10 | "With enough analysis I can predict the market." | Nobody predicts; professionals bet on asymmetry and manage probability. Prediction is astrology with charts. |
| 11 | "Demo trading is useless, I need real money to learn." | Forward-testing at minimum size is how you verify execution before it costs you. Skip it and you pay full tuition. |
| 12 | "News events are easy money." | Slippage makes your planned exit price fiction. News volatility is randomness with your name on it. |
| 13 | "Martingale/doubling after loss recovers everything." | It works until the one streak that wipes the account — and that streak is guaranteed. |
| 14 | "I'll just follow someone's calls and get rich." | Without process, every call's loss becomes your revenge trade. Calls without skill = borrowed confidence, returned with interest. |
| 15 | "Trading is easy money." | It's a skill business with a brutal attrition rate. The 30 days you just finished put you on the surviving side of that math. |

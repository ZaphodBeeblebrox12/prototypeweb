# 🎁 BONUS 4 — Curated Resources (the short list that matters)

## Books (in reading order)
1. **Trading in the Zone** — Mark Douglas. The psychology bible. Read AFTER Day 25 —
   you'll feel personally described on every page.
2. **The Disciplined Trader** — Mark Douglas. The prequel; deeper on why process beats outcome.
3. **Market Wizards** (series) — Jack Schwager. Interviews with the best ever. Notice:
   none of them trade alike; ALL of them obsess over risk.
4. **Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets** — John Murphy. The reference for
   everything in Level 2.
5. **Japanese Candlestick Charting** — Steve Nison. Only after Day 11, so you can filter
   the 5 useful patterns from the 45 decorative ones.

## Free tools
- **TradingView** — charting; free tier is enough for this course.
- **Investing.com / Economic calendar** — mark high-impact events; respect the news
  blackout (Day 26).
- **NSE website** — lot sizes, margin calculators, market-wide data. Always check the
  OFFICIAL lot size before sizing a trade.

## Ongoing practice
- One annotated chart per week, posted to your own notes — the Day 1 "watch the auction"
  exercise, forever.
- Rework one old journal trade per week: knowing what you know now, what would you have
  done? (This one habit compounds faster than any new strategy.)

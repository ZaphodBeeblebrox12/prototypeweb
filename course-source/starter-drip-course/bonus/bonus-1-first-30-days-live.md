# 🎁 BONUS 1 — Your First 30 Days Live (the week-by-week plan after graduation)

The course gives you the system. This plan gives you the *sequencing* — what to do in
your first live month so you build skill without building scars.

## Week 1 — Process before profits
- **Trade minimum size only** (1 lot / smallest quantity). The goal is reps, not money.
- ONE setup only — your best backtested one (Day 28). Nothing else is tradable this week.
- Journal every trade the same day, 2 minutes (template included).
- Daily routine: 15 min prep (bias + zones + alerts) → trade only planned levels →
  10 min review after close.
- **Success metric for the week:** 100% checklist adherence. P&L is irrelevant. If you
  took even one off-plan trade, the week is not a success — no matter the profit.

## Week 2 — Add the feedback loop
- Continue minimum size, one setup.
- **First weekly review** (Sunday, 20 min): compute the 5 metrics from Day 27.
  Expectancy won't be meaningful yet — look at *checklist adherence* and *mistake codes*.
- Fix exactly ONE thing for week 3. Write it in the playbook version log.
- If you have 10+ journaled trades: compare your live R results vs your backtest. Within
  ±30%? Your execution is fine. Way off? Audit slippage, timing, and skipped boxes.

## Week 3 — Scale carefully or stay put
- If checklist adherence ≥ 90% for two straight weeks: increase size by **25%**. Not 50%,
  not double — 25%.
- If adherence < 90%: stay at minimum size another week. There's no prize for scaling
  into a broken process.
- You may now add a SECOND setup — but only if the first is at 20+ journaled trades with
  positive expectancy. Two systems, half size each, is the ceiling for month one.

## Week 4 — First monthly review
- 60 minutes. Full month on one page: trades, R curve, metrics, mistakes, lessons.
- Compare against your playbook v1.0: which sections were right? Which were fantasy?
  Update to v1.1 with ONE change.
- Decide next month's size from the DATA: positive expectancy → hold or +25% · negative
  → back to minimum size and backtest the weak spots. Never decide from how you feel.

## The daily routine card (print this)
```
BEFORE MARKET: bias (daily) → mark zones → set alerts → write one-liners for candidates
DURING:          trade ONLY alert triggers · checklist all 10 boxes · circuit breaker armed
AFTER:           journal every trade · note feelings in one line · 10 min review
WEEKLY (Sun):    20-min review · 5 metrics · ONE tweak · version the playbook
```

## Emergency protocols (memorize)
- 3 losses or −3% → **done for the day**. No exceptions, no "one small recovery trade."
- Urge to oversize after a win → size from the formula, period. The formula doesn't know
  you're on a heater.
- Urge to re-enter instantly after a stop-out → 10-minute rule, then a NEW trigger only.

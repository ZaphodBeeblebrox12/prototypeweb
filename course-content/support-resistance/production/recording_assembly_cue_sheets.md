## Global assembly rules
- Follow the canonical script text; cue descriptions below are staging instructions, not replacement narration.
- All synthetic chart images must retain the label **“Illustrative chart — not historical market data.”** Do not assign them a real instrument/date or present them as an instructor trade.
- Never show reference annotations, later candles, outcome, trade management or quiz answers before the specified reveal/submission gate.
- Keep learner response/prompt on screen long enough to read; insert a deliberate pause before any reveal.
- Preserve the learner’s earlier response through the reveal so comparison is about reasoning, not memory.
- On mobile, show chart first and put the prompt directly below; no tiny side-by-side panels for essential chart evidence.
- Personal case dependencies remain unfilled: Lesson 9 authentic micro-case; Lessons 16–17 authentic case records.
- All durations marked estimated or sequence-based should be conformed during recording/edit; reconcile total runtime in second pass.

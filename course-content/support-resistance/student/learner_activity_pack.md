# TradeThesis — Support & Resistance
## Learner Activity Pack v1

**Status:** first-pass student-facing draft. This pack contains tasks and response prompts, not answer keys, internal asset IDs or instructor production notes. Chart examples are illustrative unless explicitly identified as an authentic instructor case.

## How to use these activities

1. Read the prompt before asking for the reference drawing.
2. Describe visible evidence before interpreting it.
3. Use conditional language when the next path is uncertain.
4. “Unclear” and “stand aside” are valid when you explain why.
5. After each reveal, note what changed in your reading and what did not.

**Important:** a marked zone is not a trade signal or a promise about future price. You are practising chart interpretation and decision structure, not receiving trading recommendations.

## Activities

### Lesson 01 — What support and resistance actually tell you

**Purpose:** Recognise prior reaction areas without treating them as promises.

**Before the reveal**

Look at each unmarked chart. Identify a broad area where price previously paused, slowed or turned. Then distinguish what the chart shows from what remains unknown.

**Record your response**

1. What repeated or related behaviour do you notice?
2. Which broad area would you mark, if any?
3. What does the area tell you about prior behaviour?
4. What can it not promise about the next move?

**After the reveal**

Write one sentence completing each prompt:

- “My first read was based on …”
- “The new evidence changed / did not change my read because …”
- “I am still uncertain about …”

**Workbook:** Chart Reading Notes

Follow the staged chart reveals provided in each lesson. Submit your first response before requesting the reference view.

### Lesson 18 — Final chart review and knowledge check

**Purpose:** Synthesize structure, zones, evidence, scenarios, invalidation and uncertainty on an unfamiliar chart.

**Before the reveal**

Work through the unseen chart in stages. Submit your full response before viewing reference analysis or the rubric details. Then complete the knowledge check.

**Record your response**

1. Describe the broad structure and supporting evidence.
2. Choose relevant zones and explain omissions.
3. Defend the upper and lower boundaries.
4. Separate observation, interpretation and uncertainty.
5. Write two conditional scenarios—or one and a justified stand-aside.
6. State invalidation and assess the next opposing area/space.

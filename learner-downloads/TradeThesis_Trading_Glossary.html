Trading glossary

Plain-English definitions for the language used throughout the Trading Path. The definitions and Indian-market context follow the supplied course glossary.

33 terms · alphabetical order

A3 terms

Ask

lowest price a seller currently accepts. You buy AT the ask.

ATM / ITM / OTM

option strike position vs current price (At/In/Out of the money).

Averaging down

adding to a losing position. Forbidden.

B3 terms

Backtest

verifying rules against historical data before risking money.

Bid

highest price a buyer currently offers. You sell AT the bid.

Breakout

price closing beyond a defined boundary (range, pattern neckline).

C2 terms

Circuit breaker (trading)

your personal daily stop: 3 losses or −3% → done.

Confluence

2+ independent tools pointing at the same price = higher-probability zone.

D2 terms

Disposition effect

cutting winners early + holding losers; loss aversion in action.

Drawdown

peak-to-trough account decline; the survival metric.

E1 term

Expectancy

average R per trade; positive after costs = you have an edge.

F3 terms

Fakeout / false breakout

breakout that instantly reverses back inside the range.

FII / DII

Foreign / Domestic Institutional Investors; the big money.

Forward test

trading a validated system at minimum size live before scaling.

L3 terms

Leverage

controlling a large position with small capital; amplifies BOTH directions.

Liquidity

how much trades without moving price; high = tight spreads, clean exits.

Lot size

minimum tradable quantity per futures/options contract (e.g., Nifty 75).

M4 terms

Margin

money your broker blocks to open a leveraged position.

Mark-to-market

daily cash settlement of futures P&L; losses debited same day.

Measured move

pattern target = breakout point ± pattern height.

MTF / Multiple Timeframe Analysis

Bias (daily) → Setup (1H) → Trigger (15m).

N1 term

Neckline

the boundary whose break confirms a double top/bottom or H&S.

O1 term

Open Interest (OI)

outstanding contracts; rising OI with rising price = fresh longs.

P4 terms

PCR (Put-Call Ratio)

sentiment gauge; extremes flag crowded positioning.

Premium

the price you pay to buy an option; can go to zero.

Pullback

temporary counter-move within a trend; the discount entry.

Pyramiding

adding only to winning positions, per pre-written plan. Allowed.

R2 terms

R-multiple (R)

profit/loss measured in units of initial risk. +2R = won 2× risked.

Revenge trading

immediate, oversized re-entry after a loss. Account-killer #1.

S3 terms

Slippage

difference between expected and actual fill price; worst in fast markets.

Spread

bid↔ask gap; a toll paid on every market-order trade.

Stop hunt

quick poke beyond a level to trigger stops before reversing; why zones have width and stops sit beyond them.

T1 term

Theta decay

options lose value as expiry approaches; the buyer's silent tax.

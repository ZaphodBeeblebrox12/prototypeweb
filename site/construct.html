The ten-week path

One step follows the last.

Open a week to see what you'll work on and what you should have in hand by Sunday.

01Context: What is the market doing?Regimes · volatility · context dashboard＋
  • Market regimes: trend, range and crisis.
  • Volatility context: ATR, India VIX and event-calendar no-trade windows.
  • Review Clinic #1: regime-map three real Nifty months.
  • Worked example: build a one-screen context dashboard.
Weekend assignment: build a context dashboard for five instruments.
02Trade construction: From observation to thesisThesis format · setup families · review＋
  • Write a thesis with a clear reason, price area, market regime and invalidation condition.
  • Compare pullback, breakout, range-fade and event setups.
  • Review Clinic #2: examine three theses and what would invalidate each.
  • Grade weak and strong theses side by side.
Weekend assignment: write five theses on live charts for rubric-based review.
03Entry engineeringTriggers · execution · missed entries＋
  • Define anticipation, confirmation and breakout triggers precisely.
  • Work through laddered entries, partial fills and Indian-market liquidity windows.
  • Set rules for missed entries and re-entry; learn when not to chase.
  • Review Clinic #3: compare three entries on the same setup in R.
Weekend assignment: complete a 20-entry drill log.
04Risk architectureInvalidation · portfolio risk · hard gate＋
  • Compare structural, volatility and time stops.
  • Map correlation clusters, concentration, gap risk and event risk.
  • Write daily and weekly loss limits into a risk ledger.
  • Build risk plans for ₹5 lakh and ₹1.5 lakh example accounts.
Mid-course gate: submit a written Risk Policy. It is reviewed before Week 5 unlocks.
05Position sizing & instrument selectionFutures · cash · options structures＋
  • Size futures, cash and options structures using volatility-aware methods.
  • Match the instrument to the thesis.
  • Plan additions to winning positions before the trade begins.
  • Review Clinic #4: compare five theses and five instrument choices.
Weekend assignment: sizing drills and instrument-selection notes for five live setups.
5BThe intraday & expiry arenaSession map · gamma · survival rules＋
  • Study the structure of an Indian trading day and the mathematics behind common intraday trades.
  • Work through expiry-day gamma, theta and pinning as an observation rather than a rule.
  • Set an expiry protocol: reduced risk, defined-risk structures, no new trades in the final hour, and skip event expiries.
Weekend assignment: complete an observation week and a paper-traded expiry under the protocol. Grading focuses on following the plan, not P&L.
06Trade management IAfter-entry decision tree · partial exits · trailing＋
  • Write if/then decisions for targets, stalls, gaps, news and structure breaks.
  • Understand partial exits, runners and the expectancy maths of scaling out.
  • Compare structure, ATR and moving-average trailing methods.
  • Review Clinic #5: manage one real trade three ways and compare outcomes.
Weekend assignment: build your personal After-Entry Decision Tree.
07Trade management IIBad market days · in-trade psychology · hedges＋
  • Plan responses for gaps through stops, circuit locks and slippage.
  • Recognise tilt while holding a position and distinguish rule-based exits from fear.
  • Study protective puts and collars, including their costs and trade-offs.
  • Review Clinic #6: compare a disaster trade's plan with what actually happened.
Weekend assignment: log responses to five adverse scenarios against your decision tree.
08Exit engineeringTargets · R-multiples · re-entry rules＋
  • Choose structural targets, R-multiple targets, time exits or thesis-change exits for the setup.
  • Separate a valid continuation from revenge trading.
  • Review Clinic #7: compare planned and emotional exits.
  • Add exit rules to your playbook.
Weekend assignment: write exit rules for each setup family.
09Review system & strategy validationJournaling · backtesting · evidence＋
  • Use a seven-step review, journal schema, weekly metrics and a mistake taxonomy.
  • Run a 50-trade backtest with costs and slippage; assess expectancy, drawdown and losing streaks.
  • Build a Strategy Card from your evidence.
  • Review Clinic #8: audit the backtest and identify ways results can be distorted.
Weekend assignment: complete a backtest report for one setup.
10Capstone: Your trading systemPlaybook · go-live plan · defence＋
  • Assemble two or three strategies with a risk policy and decision tree.
  • Plan capital staging in 25% steps and a month-one review schedule.
  • Present a recorded walkthrough assessed against construction, risk, management and review criteria.
  • Complete graduation and, optionally, share the playbook with the graduate group.
Final deliverable: a written, backtested trading system you can explain and defend.

Before you decide

A few useful questions.

Is CONSTRUCT self-paced or instructor-led?

Both. You study the core lessons independently, then work through assignments and reviews. You’ll also learn with me in live lessons: I’ll answer questions, work through charts and demonstrate how I approach execution, position management and post-trade review.

Do I need to finish the 30-day Trading Path first?

The programme is designed for people who have completed the Trading Path or can already read a chart and understand the basics. If chart reading is still new, start with the 30-day Trading Path first.

How much time should I set aside?

The programme is designed around roughly 45 minutes a day, with larger assignments intended for weekends. Your actual time will depend on how much chart practice and review you do.

Why is the programme paper-trading-led?

Your own practice is paper-trading-led, so you can experiment with setups, instruments, entries, exits and management styles, compare decisions and keep a record before risking capital. In live lessons, I demonstrate how I approach execution and management; the aim is to understand a process, not copy trade calls. Paper results still cannot guarantee live results.

What if my trading account is small?

You can complete the capstone through paper trading. Do not increase your capital just to participate. The work is about building and testing a process, not proving that you can trade a larger account.

Does this promise profits or a winning strategy?

No. The goal is to build, backtest, explain and defend a written process. Historical testing and a clear plan do not guarantee future results.

How much does CONSTRUCT cost? Is the community extra?

CONSTRUCT costs ₹17,000. Twelve months of The Wobbling Candle community are included in that fee, with no separate community charge. The next cohort date should be confirmed before enrolment opens.

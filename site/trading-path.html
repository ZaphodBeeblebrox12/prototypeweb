A small taste of the teaching
Sample from Day 1 · FoundationsHow markets actually work

A market is an auction. The price on your screen is the price of the most recent agreement between one buyer and one seller.

A buyer may accept the seller’s asking price; a seller may accept the buyer’s bid. Those decisions are part of how trades happen and why prices update. The lesson starts with market mechanics before moving on to chart tools or setups.

Try this, without placing a trade: Open Nifty 50 on a 5-minute chart and observe it for 10 minutes. Notice where price stalls and where it moves. Write down what you observed, separately from what you think it means.

The full lesson continues with key takeaways, a quick check and an action step. This is a short preview, not the complete paid lesson.

Before you decide

A few things worth knowing.

Know the shape of the course and the limits of what it can do before you spend your time or money.

Do I enrol in the stages separately?

No. The TradeThesis Trading Path is one course with one enrolment. Foundations, Technical Toolkit, Trading Mechanics, and Psychology & Going Live are connected stages within the same curriculum.

What is included in the enrolment?

The course contains 30 written lessons, action steps and knowledge checks; stage checkpoints and a final quiz; six cheat sheets and a glossary; a journal template, position-size calculator, printable checklist, 30-day tracker and playbook template; plus the bonus quiz bank and trade-review cards.

How is the course paced and delivered?

The planned format is one lesson each day through Telegram. The source course brief specifies lifetime access, so the 30-day schedule is a suggested pace rather than an access deadline; you can catch up after a missed day.

How much time should I set aside each day?

The course FAQ suggests 15–20 minutes for a lesson, followed by around 10 minutes for the action step.

What if I miss a day?

The course brief specifies lifetime access. The 30-day drip is the suggested pace, not a deadline to finish every lesson in 30 consecutive days.

Which markets does it cover?

The concepts are intended to be market-agnostic, while the examples focus on Indian instruments such as Nifty, Bank Nifty, NSE stocks and futures.

Are live classes included?

No. The Trading Path is the self-paced written course. Trade Construction & Management is a separate six-week live classroom offering.

Do I need a demat account to begin?

No. The first two levels can be studied with a free charting app. The source course says a demat account is not needed until the Level 3 action steps.

Do I need trading experience?

No prior experience is assumed. The course begins with market mechanics and the information on a trading screen, then moves step by step into analysis, planning and review.

Will it tell me what to buy or sell?

No. TradeThesis is built around education and decision-making, not a signal-copying service. Examples explain reasoning; they are not recommendations to trade.

Will this make me profitable?

No course can guarantee trading profits. Markets involve risk, and learning a process does not remove that risk.

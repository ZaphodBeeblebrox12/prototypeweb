The TradeThesis approach, in conversation

No two people
see the same chart.

TradeThesis · The whole decision

Bring the thesis.
Examine the reasoning.

A trade idea becomes more useful when its reasoning can be examined. Start with context, make the thesis explicit, test risk and management, then review what held up. Members can add a perspective at any point in that connected decision.

The conversation moves through four questions

A note from the desk

There’s no secret handshake. And no stupid question.

“I don’t want you to copy my trades. I want you to understand the thinking behind them.”

That’s the point of the lesson.

Maybe you’ve watched hours of videos and still freeze when a chart is in front of you. Maybe you know a few patterns but don’t know how to turn them into a plan. Or maybe you’re curious, but trading education has felt a little too loud, a little too certain, and a little too eager to sell you the next secret.

You can start without pretending you know more than you do.

TradeThesis is built around the whole decision. We look at context, construct a thesis, think about entry, define risk, manage the trade, decide how to exit, and review what happened. We make the reasoning visible—including the parts where there isn’t a perfect answer.

The goal isn’t to make uncertainty disappear. It’s to help you meet it with a process instead of a guess.

Come curious. Leave with better questions.

The people make the room

A place to think out loud.

Trading can get lonely when every chart feels like a test you’re supposed to pass. Learning beside others makes it easier to ask, compare and improve.

☕ Pull up a chair
For the question-askers

Bring your questions, not a perfect track record.

Talk through what you’re learning, compare how you read a chart and ask someone to explain the bit that hasn’t clicked yet. You don’t have to perform expertise to belong.

✎ Work in progress
For the long game

Small improvements count, too.

Learn to record your reasoning, notice what you missed and listen to another perspective. The aim is better judgement over time—not louder predictions.

Disagree with the idea, not the person. Nobody needs a winning trade to earn a seat at the table.

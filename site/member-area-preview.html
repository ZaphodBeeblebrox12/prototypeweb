Common questions

A few useful answers before you need to ask.

How do I link Telegram to my member account?

The production flow should start a verification request and ask you to confirm through the official TradeThesis bot. Only after successful verification should the dashboard show it as linked or grant member access.

Why connect Discord?

Discord linking can verify your account and synchronize the correct member role. Authorizations should request only the minimum permissions needed to establish the link and apply the role.

Where can I find my invoice?

Open Membership & billing, locate a payment in your invoice history and choose View receipt. The live system should display records supplied by the payment provider or billing database.

Will this dashboard renew my course automatically?

The current Trading Path offer describes lifetime access. A recurring plan, if offered in future, should show its price, renewal date and cancellation terms clearly before a member confirms payment.

Are these buttons connected to my accounts?

No. This is an interactive design prototype. It does not connect to Telegram or Discord, process payments, save profile changes or authenticate users.

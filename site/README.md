# TradeThesis public-site review build

Open `00_OPEN_INTERACTIVE_PREVIEW.html` to switch between the homepage, courses, Trading Path, CONSTRUCT and live classroom. `MOBILE_PREVIEW.html` opens the homepage at mobile dimensions.

The current site presents three distinct routes: the ₹999 30-day Trading Path; CONSTRUCT, a 70-day guided self-paced programme with assignments, review clinics, thesis review and risk audits; and the separate six-week live classroom. CONSTRUCT is priced at ₹17,000, including 12 months of The Wobbling Candle community access at no separate fee. A next cohort date is not stated in the public copy.

All pages use the original TradeThesis horizontal logo and preserve relative `assets/` references. This is a static design preview: payment, enrolment and enquiry delivery are not connected.

01 / SUPPORT

The two lows

The troughs form near a similar price area, even though the wick lows are not identical. A zone captures that variation better than a single exact line.

02 / RESISTANCE

The neckline

The swing high between the two troughs marks an area where the recovery previously stalled. The later move above it is a break through that resistance area in this sample.

03 / THE IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Mark evidence, not certainty

Drawing zones does not create support or resistance. Zones are how we mark areas where price has previously reacted. Neither guarantees a bounce, a breakout or a trade.

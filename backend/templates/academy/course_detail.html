A PRACTICAL LEARNING RHYTHM

Notice. Compare. Apply. Review.

Each lesson should help you see what matters, weigh alternatives, use the idea in context and review the reasoning—not simply memorise a label.

01 · NOTICE

Observe

Describe what is visible before deciding what it means.

02 · COMPARE

Consider alternatives

Look for evidence that supports or weakens an interpretation.

03 · APPLY

Use the idea

Practise the lesson in context and identify what would invalidate it.

04 · REVIEW

Reflect

Record what you learned and what you would check next time.

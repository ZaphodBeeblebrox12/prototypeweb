Before you submit

Include the steps you took, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. For payment issues, include the approximate date and plan name. Do not share passwords, full card details, or one-time codes.

What happens next?

  2. Keep the ticket reference so you can return to the same request.
  3. Check the ticket detail page for its current status and any review note.

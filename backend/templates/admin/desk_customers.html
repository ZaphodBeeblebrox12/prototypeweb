Reviewer scope

Support counts and ticket links are calculated from the tickets this reviewer can access. Customer 360 applies additional, independent permissions to subscriptions, payments, invoices, Academy and security records.

No cross-customer ticket or financial records are added to this page outside their owning permission scope.

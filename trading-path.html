A small taste of the teaching
A short practice promptWhat does the price on your screen actually tell you?

The last traded price records the most recent executed trade. It is useful information, but it is not a promise about the next price and it does not tell the whole story of the order book.

Try separating observation from interpretation. “The last trade was at 24,100” is an observation. “Buyers are in control and price must rise” is an interpretation that needs more evidence.

A question to practise: What else would you want to check before deciding what this price movement means?

Before you decide

A few things worth knowing.

Know the shape of the course and the limits of what it can do before you spend your time or money.

Do I enrol in the stages separately?

No. The TradeThesis Trading Path is one course with one enrolment. Foundations, Technical Toolkit, Trading Mechanics, and Psychology & Going Live are connected stages within the same curriculum.

What is included in the enrolment?

The course contains 30 written lessons across the four stages, practice prompts and knowledge checks, stage quizzes, the Foundations Field Notes workbook, teaching diagrams, a fictional case exercise, checklist and playbook-building practice, and a final quiz.

How is the course paced?

The curriculum is designed as a 30-day sequence with one lesson per day. Automated release and learner access need to be connected in the production course system before launch.

Do I need trading experience?

No prior experience is assumed. The course begins with market mechanics and the information on a trading screen, then moves step by step into analysis, planning and review.

Will it tell me what to buy or sell?

No. TradeThesis is built around education and decision-making, not a signal-copying service. Examples explain reasoning; they are not recommendations to trade.

Will this make me profitable?

No course can guarantee trading profits. Markets involve risk, and learning a process does not remove that risk.
